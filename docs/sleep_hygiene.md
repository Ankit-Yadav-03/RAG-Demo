# Sleep Hygiene Notes

Most adults need between seven and nine hours of sleep per night. Consistently sleeping fewer than six hours is linked to impaired attention and memory.

Keeping a regular sleep schedule, including weekends, helps stabilise the body's circadian rhythm. The bedroom should be cool, dark and quiet; around 18 degrees Celsius is often recommended.

Caffeine has a half-life of roughly five to six hours, so afternoon coffee can still affect sleep at night. Screens emit blue light that can suppress melatonin, so avoid them for an hour before bed.
