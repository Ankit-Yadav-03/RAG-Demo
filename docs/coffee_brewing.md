# Coffee Brewing Guide

The golden ratio for drip coffee is roughly 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water. For a 500 ml pot, that is about 31 grams of ground coffee.

Water temperature matters: aim for 90 to 96 degrees Celsius. Boiling water scorches the grounds and produces bitterness, while water that is too cool gives sour, under-extracted coffee.

Grind size should match the method. Espresso needs a fine grind, pour-over a medium grind, and French press a coarse grind. A French press should steep for about four minutes.

Coffee beans are best used within two to four weeks of the roast date. Store them in an airtight container away from light and heat.
